01

Overview

Flexana ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Flexana mobile application — a yoga and Reformer Pilates class booking platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and safeguard your personal data when you use our app.

By using Flexana, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

02

Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data when you use Flexana:

Data Type Description Format
Full Name Provided during account registration Hashed (SHA-256)
Phone Number Used for account verification and contact Hashed (SHA-256)
Email Address Used for login and notifications Hashed (SHA-256)
View Content Classes and pages browsed within the app Event
Search Search terms entered in the app Event
Initiate Checkout When a booking or purchase flow begins Event
Purchase Completed class or package bookings Event
Crash Reports Automatic diagnostics to improve app stability System

Personal identifiers (name, phone, email) are hashed using SHA-256 before being transmitted to any analytics or advertising service — meaning the raw values never leave your device in plain text.

04

Third-Party Services

Flexana integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

Service Purpose
Firebase (Google) Authentication, analytics, push notifications (FCM), and crash reporting (Crashlytics)
Meta (Facebook) Conversions API Server-side ad conversion tracking using hashed user data
Paymob Payment processing for class bookings and packages

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Hashed identifiers shared with Meta are used solely for conversion measurement and cannot be reverse-engineered into your original information.

05

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Crash and analytics data may be retained in anonymized form for up to 24 months.

Rectification

Opt-Out
Opt out of ad tracking at any time via device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

07

Children's Privacy

Flexana is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete it.
